📜 Rules

1. Host makes a room, friends join with the code (2 to 6 teams).
2. Only the host presses Next, Sold, Unsold, Pause and Undo. Host can bid too.
3. Every bid resets the timer to at least 8 sec. You cannot outbid yourself. No bids after time ends or while paused.
4. Buy exactly the squad size. Squad full = no more bids. Fewer players at the end = ❌ eliminated.
5. Keep base price for every empty slot. Max bid = purse − base × empty slots.
6. Trade: ask for another team's player, or offer your own, with a price. The other team must accept.
7. Mystery players stay hidden until the first bid.
8. Winner = highest total OVR of the whole squad (+10 for captain). 90+ OVR is rare, 👑 legends are the best.
9. Pass button: tap it if you are not interested. After the auction, open the AI page to compare squads.
